1. Product Overview

Viczuals is a creative visual content studio that helps brands, businesses, and creators tell powerful stories through high-quality visual content. Its core services are video editing, motion graphics, graphic design, branding, and digital content creation.

This project is a single marketing landing page that clearly communicates Viczuals’ capabilities, demonstrates the quality and range of its work, and turns interested visitors into qualified enquiries. The page is not a client portal, ecommerce experience, booking system, or content platform.

## 2. Background and Brand Foundation

### Mission

Help brands, businesses, and creators tell powerful stories through high-quality visual content that inspires engagement, builds credibility, and drives growth.

### Vision

Become a leading creative studio recognized for transforming ideas into impactful visual experiences across digital and traditional platforms.

### Brand personality

Confident, imaginative, modern, collaborative, energetic, and polished. The experience should signal creative craft without feeling cluttered or derivative.

## 3. Objectives

### Primary objective

Make it effortless for prospective clients to initiate a project conversation with Viczuals.

### Secondary objectives

- Establish Viczuals as a capable, credible creative partner.
- Explain the studio’s service offering in language prospective clients can quickly understand.
- Showcase selected work and creative range.
- Give visitors enough context to decide whether Viczuals is right for their project.

### Success signals

- Visitors use the primary contact CTA or submit the project enquiry form.
- Visitors view work samples and service details before contacting the studio.
- Enquiries include enough project context for a timely, useful response.

## 4. Target Audience

### Primary audiences

1. Small and growing businesses that need professional brand and digital content support.
2. Marketing teams and founders seeking an external creative partner for campaigns, social content, or brand work.
3. Creators, personal brands, and media-led businesses that need visually strong, consistent content.

### Audience needs

- See relevant work and services quickly.
- Understand what Viczuals can create and how collaboration works.
- Feel confident in the studio’s taste, reliability, and professionalism.
- Contact the studio without account creation or unnecessary friction.

## 5. Positioning and Key Message

Viczuals transforms ideas into compelling visual experiences across video, motion, design, branding, and digital content.

The page should make this promise tangible: the studio combines creative thinking and production craft to create visual work that helps audiences notice, trust, and engage with a brand.

## 6. Scope

### In scope

- One responsive, public-facing landing page.
- Navigation to page anchors and approved external contact/social links.
- Service summaries, selected work, studio introduction, and project enquiry.
- A validated contact form that sends enquiries to the studio’s approved inbox or form endpoint.
- Basic measurement, SEO metadata, performance, and accessibility support.

### Out of scope

- User accounts, logins, client dashboards, and project tracking.
- Online checkout, payments, quotes, or scheduling workflows.
- A full CMS, blog, resource library, or multi-page portfolio.
- Live chat, AI assistant, testimonials collection, or job application workflows.
- Complex filtering, portfolio search, or animation that compromises performance or usability.

## 7. Sitemap / Information Architecture

The landing page uses a single-page structure with anchor navigation.

| Navigation label | Anchor / destination | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work | `#work` | Show selected projects and creative output. |
| Services | `#services` | Explain capabilities and relevant deliverables. |
| About | `#about` | Introduce the studio’s mission and point of view. |
| Contact | `#contact` | Start a project enquiry. |
| Start a project | `#contact` | Persistent primary conversion action. |

The Viczuals logo links to the top of the page. The footer repeats key navigation and includes approved contact and social links.

## 8. Page Sections

### 8.1 Header

- Display the Viczuals logo at left and anchor navigation at right.
- Include a visually prominent primary CTA: **Start a project**.
- Keep the header usable at all viewport sizes; on small screens, provide an accessible menu control or a compact navigation treatment.
- A sticky header is optional, provided it does not obscure anchor targets or consume excessive screen space.

### 8.2 Hero

Purpose: immediately communicate what Viczuals does and guide visitors to either explore work or make contact.

- Use an original, studio-specific headline; do not mirror or closely imitate any reference copy or layout.
- Suggested message direction: “Visual stories built to move your brand forward.”
- Include a concise supporting statement covering visual content, creative craft, and business impact.
- Include primary CTA **Start a project** linking to `#contact` and secondary CTA **View our work** linking to `#work`.
- Use an original hero visual, motion reel still, or abstract composition that reflects the studio’s capabilities. It must not depend on a third-party reference design.

### 8.3 Selected Work (`#work`)

Purpose: provide visual proof of quality and range.

- Present 3–6 featured projects in a responsive grid or editorial layout.
- Each item includes a thumbnail or video preview, project/client name (where approved), service category, and a brief one-line outcome or description.
- Clicking an item may open an accessible lightbox or lead to an approved external case-study/reel link. If no destination exists, items must not be styled as non-functional links.
- Use optimized imagery and provide meaningful alt text; decorative imagery may use empty alt text.
- Include a CTA after the gallery: **Have a project in mind? Let’s talk.**

### 8.4 Services (`#services`)

Purpose: make the offering easy to scan and easy to match to a visitor’s need.

Show the following five services as distinct cards or clearly separated blocks:

| Service | Example deliverables |
| --- | --- |
| Video Editing | Social edits, campaign videos, interviews, reels, short-form and long-form edits. |
| Motion Graphics | Animated titles, explainers, logo animation, social motion assets, visual effects. |
| Graphic Design | Campaign artwork, print and digital graphics, social assets, presentations, marketing materials. |
| Branding | Brand identity systems, logos, visual guidelines, brand applications, launch collateral. |
| Digital Content Creation | Content concepts, social media creative, launch assets, platform-ready content packages. |

- Each service block includes a plain-language description and 3–5 example deliverables.
- Avoid displaying unconfirmed pricing, turnaround guarantees, or service claims.
- Include a contextual CTA that scrolls to the contact form.

### 8.5 About Viczuals (`#about`)

Purpose: establish perspective and trust without becoming a long company history.

- Present a concise studio introduction grounded in the mission and vision.
- Explain the collaborative value proposition: turning ideas into visually impactful work for digital and traditional platforms.
- Include a compact “How we work” sequence: **Discover → Create → Refine → Deliver**. This is explanatory only; it is not a project management promise or formal process guarantee.
- Optional: include a studio/founder image only if approved and available.

### 8.6 Contact / Start a Project (`#contact`)

Purpose: convert interest into a qualified, low-friction enquiry.

- Lead with an inviting heading such as “Let’s make something worth watching.”
- Include a short expectation-setting note: Viczuals will respond through the preferred contact details provided.
- Show the project enquiry form described in Section 10.
- Display direct contact options (email and phone/WhatsApp) only when final details are approved. All direct contact links must work on supported devices.
- Reiterate one clear submission CTA: **Send project enquiry**.

### 8.7 Footer

- Include logo/wordmark, concise studio descriptor, page anchors, and approved social links.
- Include copyright text with the current year generated or maintained appropriately.
- Include privacy link only if a privacy notice exists; do not include a dead link.

## 9. Functional Requirements

### Navigation and interaction

- Anchor links scroll to their respective sections and place the section heading below any sticky header.
- Keyboard users can reach and operate every interactive element in a logical order.
- A visible keyboard focus indicator is required.
- Buttons and links must have unambiguous labels; the logo’s accessible label is “Viczuals home.”
- Any carousel, autoplaying video, or motion effect must be optional or controllable. A static layout is preferred for project work.

### Work media

- Video previews must not autoplay with sound.
- Provide a poster image and accessible label for video controls or external video links.
- Use a lightweight loading strategy for media below the fold.
